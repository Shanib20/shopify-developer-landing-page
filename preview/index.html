Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check my order status?

You'll receive a tracking link by email once your order ships. For anything else, reach out via the Contact page.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — see the Shipping page for estimated delivery windows by region.

How do I care for embroidered pieces?

Dry clean only, stored flat or on a padded hanger, away from direct sunlight to preserve the threadwork.

Can I cancel or change my order?

Contact us within 2 hours of placing your order and we'll do our best to amend it before it's processed.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Gift wrapping can be requested at checkout for a small additional fee.

About Heritage

Heritage began as an idea: that the techniques behind embroidery, weaving and metalwork passed down through generations deserve a place in how people dress today, not just in archives.

Every collection pairs traditional craft — zardozi threadwork, kundan setting, hand-loomed textiles — with silhouettes built for how people actually live and dress now.

Sustainability

Demo content for this interview project. A live store would describe its actual sourcing and certifications here rather than general statements.

We favor natural fibers — silk, cotton and leather — chosen for how they wear and age, not just how they photograph on day one.

Where possible, we work with artisan groups who specialize in specific regional techniques, aiming to keep that craft economically viable rather than treating it as a one-off motif.
